eND Rev A-1 — Schematic Partitions and Signal-Level Notes
Cinema phone conversion, Sony LYT-900 / Xiaomi 14 Ultra
================================================================

OVERVIEW
────────
The Rev A-1 driver board is partitioned into five schematic sheets, each
independently reviewable and independently testable on the bench.

  Sheet  Name                  Key devices
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1      Power                 TPS65131, LDOs, bulk caps, ferrite EMI
  2      MCU and comms         STM32U5, USB-CDC, UART, programming header
  3      Sensors and DAC       TMP117, AD5696R, I²C, SPI
  4      Analog output stage   OPA2197, ADG1419, AC drive generation
  5      Flex interface        Connector, ESD, filter, test points
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────


SHEET 1: POWER
──────────────

  Input voltage:   4.2–5.5 V (USB VBUS from phone or grip battery)

  TPS65131 (split-rail boost/inverting regulator)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────────
  Input: VIN = 4.2–5.5 V
  Output 1: +VDRV = +15.0 V, 200 mA max
  Output 2: −VDRV = −15.0 V, 200 mA max (inverted output path)
  Switching frequency: set to 1.2 MHz (spread-spectrum if available in
  this variant) — must not land on MIPI D-PHY clock harmonics for
  the main camera module (confirm MIPI lane frequency before layout)

  Output capacitors: 10 µF + 100 nF X7R each rail, minimum 25 V rating
  EMI suppression: ferrite bead (Murata BLM18PG221SN1 or equivalent,
  100 Ω @ 100 MHz) on each output rail before distribution

  +3.3 V LDO (MCU and digital supply)
  ────────────────────────────────────
  Input: 5 V from USB VBUS
  Output: 3.3 V, 200 mA (MCU + TMP117 + AD5696R digital)
  Part: TI TLV757P or equivalent, SOT-23-5
  Bypass: 10 µF + 100 nF at output

  +3.3 V_ANA LDO (precision analog supply)
  ──────────────────────────────────────────
  Separate LDO for AD5696R AVDD and OPA2197 supply domain
  Input: 5 V
  Output: 3.3 V, 100 mA
  Part: TI REF5030 used as precision reference for AD5696R, OR
        dedicated AVDD LDO if AD5696R internal reference is used
  Purpose: isolate DAC and op-amp supply from MCU switching noise
  Bypass: 10 µF + 100 nF at output, additional 10 nF at each IC AVDD pin

  Test points on Sheet 1:
    TP_VIN, TP_VDRV_P, TP_VDRV_N, TP_3V3D, TP_3V3A, TP_GND

  Design notes:
  - Stitch GND plane with vias aggressively beneath TPS65131
  - Separate analog GND polygon for OPA2197 / DAC domain; single-point
    connection to digital GND at MCU GND pad
  - Shield can over TPS65131 if EMI into MIPI lanes observed in Rev A-0
  - Place bulk cap (47 µF ceramic or 100 µF tantalum) at board power entry


SHEET 2: MCU AND COMMS
───────────────────────

  STM32U5 (supervisor MCU)
  ─────────────────────────
  Recommended part: STM32U575CIT6 (UFBGA132, 7×7 mm)
  Alternatively for smaller board: STM32U535CEU6 (UFQFPN48, 7×7 mm)
  Supply: 3.3 V (Sheet 1)
  Crystal: 16 MHz HSE, CL = 10 pF, ESR < 50 Ω
           (required for USB full-speed operation in Rev A-0 bench mode)
  LSE: 32.768 kHz if RTC needed for logging timestamps

  Peripheral assignments (Rev A-1):
  ──────────────────────────────────
    USART1      : Android host bridge (UART 115200–1000000 baud, 8N1)
                  Pins: PA9 (TX), PA10 (RX)
                  RS-level: 3.3 V LVTTL direct if MCU is in mod grip;
                  add Si8621 isolator if ground loops with phone mainboard
                  are a concern

    USB_OTG_FS  : Rev A-0 bench mode host bridge (USB-CDC)
                  Pins: PA11 (DM), PA12 (DP)
                  ESD: PRTR5V0U2X or equivalent on each data line

    SPI1        : AD5696R DAC
                  Pins: PA5 (SCK), PA7 (MOSI), PA4 (NSS/CS)
                  Frequency: 10 MHz max (AD5696R Fsclk max 50 MHz;
                  limit to 10 MHz for first-article EMI margin)

    I²C1        : TMP117 temperature sensor
                  Pins: PB6 (SCL), PB7 (SDA)
                  Pullups: 4.7 kΩ to 3.3 V
                  Address: TMP117 ADD0 pin to GND → 0x48

    GPIO out    : ADG1419 switch control (PA0)
                  GPIO out    : AD5696R /RESET (PA1, active low)
                  GPIO out    : status LED (green, PB0) — optional
                  GPIO in     : FAULT input from TPS65131 /PG or thermal
                                shutdown signal (PB1)

  Programming header:
    4-pin SWD: SWDIO, SWDCLK, nRST, GND
    1.27 mm pitch, Tag-Connect TC2030-IDC or Cortex-M standard 10-pin
    Accessible from board edge without disassembly

  Boot mode:
    BOOT0 tied to GND via 10 kΩ; solder bridge to 3.3 V for DFU mode
    DFU mode (USB) is the field programming path; SWD is debug/dev only


SHEET 3: SENSORS AND DAC
──────────────────────────

  TMP117 (cell temperature sensor)
  ─────────────────────────────────
  Supply: 3.3 V
  Interface: I²C (Sheet 2 I²C1 bus)
  Address: 0x48 (ADD0 = GND)
  Bypass: 100 nF + 10 nF at VCC pin
  Physical placement: This device is NOT on the control board.
    It mounts on a small flex tail attached to the eND cartridge frame
    (see Sheet 5 / flex interface).  The I²C lines route through the
    flex harness pins 5 (SDA) and 6 (SCL).
  Alert output: wire to MCU GPIO for interrupt-driven over-temperature
    detection; threshold register set in firmware to 60°C (LC cell max
    operating temperature; confirm with LC-Tec datasheet)

  AD5696R (quad 16-bit DAC)
  ─────────────────────────
  Supply: AVDD = 3.3 V_ANA (Sheet 1)
  Reference: internal 2.5 V reference (AD5696R -R suffix)
             Output range = 0–2.5 V (×2 gain option not used in Rev A)
  Interface: SPI (Sheet 2 SPI1)
  Channels in use:
    DAC A: Vsetpoint_A — setpoint for LC cell A output stage
    DAC B: Vsetpoint_B — setpoint for LC cell B output stage
    DAC C: Vcal_trim   — calibration trim (factory offset correction)
    DAC D: Vmonitor    — test output, mirrors active setpoint for debug
  /RESET: held low during power-on via RC delay (R=10kΩ, C=100nF),
          then released by MCU GPIO (Sheet 2)
  Bypass: 10 µF + 100 nF at AVDD; 100 nF at IOVCC (3.3 V digital)
  Output impedance: Rout of DAC is ~11 kΩ; buffered by OPA2197 on Sheet 4

  Signal levels at DAC outputs:
    Vsetpoint_A, Vsetpoint_B: 0–2.5 V representing 0–Vmax drive request
    Scale factor (firmware): 1 LSB = 2.5 V / 65535 ≈ 38 µV
    Effective ND resolution at output: depends on cell Vrms curve slope;
    expect ~0.01 stop per step in mid-range (more than adequate)


SHEET 4: ANALOG OUTPUT STAGE
─────────────────────────────

  Purpose: Convert DAC DC setpoints to AC drive waveforms for the LC cells.

  IMPORTANT DESIGN NOTE: The exact output stage topology must be validated
  with the actual LC-Tec cell before finalizing this sheet. The component
  choices below are based on LC-Tec published drive requirements (AC square
  wave, 0–15 VRMS, no DC offset). Revise after bench characterization of
  cell electrical behavior (capacitance, impedance vs frequency vs state).

  OPA2197 (dual precision op amp)
  ────────────────────────────────
  Supply: ±VDRV rails (+15 V / −15 V from Sheet 1)
  Configuration:
    OPA2197_A: Level-shift buffer for cell A
      - Non-inverting input: Vsetpoint_A from AD5696R
      - Gain = 1 + R2/R1 (set for 0–2.5 V in → 0–10 VRMS out range first;
        adjust after cell Vrms/T characterization)
      - Output drives ADG1419 input for AC generation

    OPA2197_B: Level-shift buffer for cell B (same topology)

  Topology note for Rev A bench phase:
    Rev A-0 uses the LCC-230 directly — this sheet is for Rev A-1 only.
    For Rev A-1, start with a simple voltage-follower on the op-amp output
    (gain = 1) and use the DAC's full 0–2.5 V range to the ADG1419.
    The ADG1419 then switches the output between +Vsetpoint and −Vsetpoint
    on alternate half-cycles to produce a bipolar AC drive.

  ADG1419 (precision SPDT analog switch)
  ───────────────────────────────────────
  Supply: +VDRV (15 V) for switch rail, logic supply 3.3 V
  Per channel (one per LC cell):
    IN_A: +Vsetpoint (buffered OPA2197 output)
    IN_B: −Vsetpoint (inverter or negative rail version)
    Control: MCU GPIO (timer PWM output, frequency configurable)
    OUT: to LC cell terminal via flex harness

  AC drive generation:
    MCU TIM1 PWM output drives ADG1419 control pin
    Frequency: configurable 10–100 Hz (firmware parameter)
    Duty cycle: 50% (enforced in firmware — any asymmetry introduces DC)
    DC offset guard: firmware monitors drive symmetry; halts drive if
      |V_avg| > 50 mV over a 10-cycle window (protects LC film from
      electrolytic degradation)

  Cell drive output spec:
    Vrms = Vsetpoint × (duty correction factor) × switch efficiency
    Maximum output: 12 VRMS (leave 3 V headroom below ±15 V rail)
    Minimum output: 0.5 VRMS (below this, drive disabled, output = 0)

  Protection:
    Series 100 Ω resistor on each cell output line (limits current during
    shorts or unexpected cell capacitance events)
    Ferrite bead on each cell output (reduces RF coupling into phone chassis)
    TVS diode at cell connector pins (Bourns CDSOD323-T05C or equivalent,
    5.0 V clamp) — note: TVS clamp must be above drive Vpeak; select
    appropriately for the actual operating Vrms after characterization

  Test points on Sheet 4:
    TP_VSET_A, TP_VSET_B: buffered DAC setpoints (DC)
    TP_DRIVE_A, TP_DRIVE_B: AC drive outputs before flex connector
    TP_VDRV_P, TP_VDRV_N: rail probe points


SHEET 5: FLEX INTERFACE
────────────────────────

  Connector: Molex 53048-0610 (PicoBlade, 6-pos, 1.25 mm, SMD)
  Mating cable: Molex 51021-0600 pre-crimped, 50 mm default length

  Pin assignments:
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  Pin  Signal          Direction    Notes
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
  1    DRIVE_A         OUT → cell   AC drive for LC cell A, 0–12 VRMS
  2    DRIVE_B         OUT → cell   AC drive for LC cell B, 0–12 VRMS
  3    DRIVE_GND       —            Drive return / shield
  4    +3V3            OUT → sensor TMP117 VCC supply, 50 mA max
  5    I2C_SDA         BIDIR        TMP117 data, 4.7 kΩ pullup on board
  6    I2C_SCL         OUT          TMP117 clock, 4.7 kΩ pullup on board
  ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

  ESD protection:
    DRIVE_A, DRIVE_B: TVS as noted in Sheet 4
    I2C_SDA, I2C_SCL: PRTR5V0U2X or ESDA6V1L (SOT-363) dual-line ESD
    +3V3: ferrite bead + 100 nF local cap at connector pin

  EMI / shield:
    Drive GND (pin 3) connected to copper shield trace in flex cable
    Shield grounded at board end only (float at cartridge end) to avoid
    ground loop through the cartridge frame into the phone chassis

  Flex cable routing requirements:
    - Keep DRIVE_A/B wires away from I²C lines (crosstalk → false TMP117
      reads at high drive frequencies; use ground-guard trace between)
    - Bend radius minimum: 2 mm (based on FPC standard 0.1 mm thickness)
    - Route away from MIPI camera flex and proximity to TPS65131

KNOWN ANALOG RISKS AND MITIGATIONS
────────────────────────────────────

  1. TPS65131 switching noise into MIPI lanes
     Risk:   1.2 MHz switcher and harmonics couple through PCB/chassis
             into camera MIPI lanes (~500 MHz MIPI D-PHY on SM8650)
     Mitigation: shield can, spread-spectrum option on regulator,
                 ferrite on VDRV outputs, keep TPS away from flex paths

  2. Drive frequency ↔ sensor rolling shutter beat frequency
     Risk:   LC drive at 50 Hz beats with 50 Hz line power and sensor
             row timing, producing a visible horizontal band
     Mitigation: firmware parameter to offset drive frequency from all
                 integer multiples of fps × shutter angle; bench-measure
                 visible flicker across all target frame rates before Rev A-1
                 layout commitment

  3. DC offset in AC drive path
     Risk:   ADG1419 charge injection or asymmetric OPA2197 offset
             introduces a residual DC component on the cell terminals;
             accelerates LC film degradation
     Mitigation: DC offset monitoring in firmware (ADC on drive output);
                 automatic null correction via DAC C trim channel;
                 hardware RC high-pass filter (C_series) may be added
                 on cell output lines if firmware correction is insufficient
                 (C_series creates I²R heating — characterize first)

  4. Ground loop through cartridge metal frame to phone chassis
     Risk:   Cartridge aluminum frame contacts phone chassis; drive return
             current finds a path through chassis and back via USB ground,
             injecting noise into phone analog references
     Mitigation: isolate cartridge frame from phone chassis electrically
                 (PEEK isolating washers at mounting points); connect DRIVE_GND
                 to phone GND only through the flex cable pin 3 (one path)

  5. Cell temperature exceeding 50°C
     Risk:   LC-Tec cells typically rated to 60–70°C but transmittance curve
             shifts with temperature; if phone chassis heat soaks cartridge
             the LUT becomes invalid
     Mitigation: TMP117 hardware alert at 55°C; firmware reduces ND range
                 until T < 50°C; thermal barrier washer between cartridge
                 frame and phone chassis (if PEEK frame not used)


LAYOUT PRIORITIES (Rev A-1 PCB)
─────────────────────────────────

  1. TPS65131 island: keep switcher and its inductors/caps in one corner,
     maximum copper poured GND beneath, away from all signal traces
  2. Analog polygon: single GND connection to digital GND at MCU
  3. Flex connector keepout: no vias within 1.5 mm of connector pads
  4. Drive trace width: 0.2 mm minimum for DRIVE_A/B on PCB before flex;
     maintain 50 Ω controlled impedance is not required here (low freq)
  5. SWD header accessible from board edge without removing shield cans
  6. Test points on all key signals, accessible with 1 mm probe tip
