eND Rev A — Mechanical Cartridge Specification
Cinema phone conversion, Sony LYT-900 / Xiaomi 14 Ultra
================================================================

DOCUMENT SCOPE
──────────────
This document defines the mechanical envelope, tolerances, and assembly
requirements for the Rev A eND optical cartridge.  Dimensions are working
targets for first-article fabrication.  All values subject to revision after
sensor module survey.

PREREQUISITE: Survey actual Xiaomi 13U/14U main camera module to measure:
  a) Available Z-clearance between lens rear element and LYT-900 cover glass
  b) Module footprint and flex routing constraints
  c) Mechanical datum surfaces for cartridge seating

Do not machine production parts before this survey.


REFERENCE FRAME
───────────────
  +Z  : lens-to-sensor direction (optic axis)
  +X  : along long axis of sensor (horizontal in landscape orientation)
  +Y  : along short axis of sensor (vertical in landscape orientation)
  Origin: center of LYT-900 active area, at sensor cover glass surface

FRAME MATERIAL AND FINISH
──────────────────────────

  Primary choice:   Black-anodized 6061-T6 aluminum
                    Anodize: Type III hard, 25 µm minimum
                    Color: matte black, Ra ≤ 1.6 µm on all non-optical faces

  Alternative:      Black PEEK (Victrex 450G or equivalent)
                    Use if thermal isolation from metal chassis is required
                    Note: PEEK has lower CTE match to the LC cell substrate
                    (typically borosilicate); verify no cell edge stress

  Inner frame surfaces (light-trap faces):
                    Light-absorbing coating, e.g. Acktar Fractal Black
                    or similar carbon-based ultra-black treatment
                    Reflectance < 0.5% at all angles > 5°

  Optical surfaces: Do not coat with black — AR windows only


FRAME FEATURES
──────────────

  1. Cell retention slots
     Two M1.2 × 0.25 mm threaded inserts (stainless), accessible from
     front (lens side).  Torque spec: 0.04 N·m maximum.
     Cell must be replaceable without removing the sensor module.

  2. Shim seats
     Minimum 3 seats, located:
       - Corner opposite flex exit (primary tilt reference)
       - Remaining two non-flex-exit corners
     Seat depth: 0.05 mm — 0.30 mm adjustable range (6 shims × 0.05 mm)
     Shim material: stainless steel or brass, lapped flat within λ/4

  3. Flex harness exit
     One edge of the frame (nominally the +X / +Y corner edge to avoid
     blocking module flex routes — confirm with physical survey).
     Exit window: 4.5 mm wide × 1.0 mm tall
     FFC/FPC connector: Molex PicoBlade 53048-0610 or equivalent
       6 contacts, 1.25 mm pitch, SMD/THM, top-contact, 1.0 mm insulation
     Connector located on inner face of frame wall, flush with cartridge rear

  4. Anti-rotation feature
     One 0.5 mm × 0.5 mm locating pin seat on rear face (sensor side) to
     prevent rotational misalignment of cartridge on reinstall.
     Corresponding pin on phone chassis mounting pad.

  5. Gasket channel
     Continuous groove, 0.8 mm wide × 0.4 mm deep, on rear face perimeter
     for an EPDM or silicone O-ring gasket.
     Purpose: dust seal between cartridge and phone chassis.
     Not hermetic — allows pressure equalization.

  6. Handling tab
     One 2.0 mm wide × 1.0 mm tall feature on one long edge (opposite flex
     exit) to allow cartridge removal with a spudger without tool-on-glass
     contact.


OPTICAL ELEMENT RETENTION
──────────────────────────

  The LC cell assembly is retained inside the frame by:
    - Cell ledge shoulder (0.3 mm) on front frame face
    - Two M1.2 retention screws through rear frame lip
    - No adhesive bonding in Rev A (bond Rev B only after cell confirmed)

  Cell-to-frame lateral clearance: 0.05 mm per side (slip fit, no bond)
  Cell-to-frame Z-axis retention: rear screws + front ledge, no gap


CLEARANCE TO SENSOR (Sony LYT-900)
───────────────────

  Minimum clearance, cartridge rear face to LYT-900 cover glass:
    Nominal:    0.15 mm
    Minimum:    0.10 mm  (hard limit — less risks contact under vibration)
    Maximum:    0.50 mm  (more than this may cause vignetting at f/4.0 edges;
                          model with ray-trace before finalizing spacer height)

  Spacer material: polished stainless steel shim or precision-ground PEEK washer


TILT SPECIFICATION
──────────────────

  Allowable cartridge tilt after shimming:  ≤ 0.10 mrad (both X and Y axes)
  Shimming procedure:
    1. Mount cartridge on chassis with all 3 shim seats engaged, zero shims
    2. Measure tilt using collimated reference beam through CA
    3. Calculate required shim stack for each seat to minimize tilt
    4. Re-measure after shim insertion — iterate until ≤ 0.10 mrad
    5. Document final shim configuration in build log

  Flatness of carrier mounting surface (chassis side): ≤ λ/2 @ 633 nm
  (polish/lap if raw machined surface exceeds this)


MASS BUDGET
───────────

  Target cartridge mass:  ≤ 0.8 g (aluminum frame + windows + cell + screws)
  Mass budget by element:
    Frame (aluminum):       ~0.30 g
    Front AR window:        ~0.10 g
    Rear AR window:         ~0.10 g
    LC cell assembly:       ~0.15 g
    Screws, shims, gasket:  ~0.05 g
    Flex connector:         ~0.05 g
    Margin:                  0.05 g
    ─────────────────────
    Total:                  ~0.80 g

INSPECTION CRITERIA (first article)
─────────────────────────────────────

  Pass / fail criteria for first-article acceptance:

  a) Outer dimensions within tolerance (CMM or calibrated caliper)
  b) Clear aperture ≥ 11.8 mm × 9.4 mm measured optically (accept slight
     under-size due to machining; undersize is recoverable, oversize is not)
  c) Frame flatness on rear face: ≤ λ/2 @ 633 nm over full aperture
  d) AR window surface quality: scratch-dig ≤ 40-20 (MIL-PRF-13830B)
  e) No visible contamination in optical path after assembly
  f) Cell retention: no lateral movement > 0.05 mm under 5 N shear
  g) Flex harness: connector seated, no delamination, continuity on all 6 pins
  h) Shim seats: accept 0.05 mm shim stock, no rattle under 10 g acceleration
  i) Tilt after zero-shim mount: record as-built value (target ≤ 1.0 mrad
     before shimming, ≤ 0.10 mrad after shimming)
  j) Mass ≤ 0.85 g (5 g margin over target)
